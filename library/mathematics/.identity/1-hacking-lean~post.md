## A Reflection on Riemann

Watching the Riemann story unfold has been fascinating. It’s humbling to imagine that we might be staring at a profound mathematical truth that we might not be able to understand. Hopefully mathematicians will be able to simplify this one extract the critical insights. But we know that proofs can be unbounded in length, so if not this proof, what about the next one, and the next? What happens ten years from now?
- [Annotation]: This reminds me of Scott Aaronson talking about the difference between classic and quantum computing. Clearly in this analogy, we humans are the classical system, and this proof is a product of the quantum realm
