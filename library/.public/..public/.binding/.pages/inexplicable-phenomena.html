What This Library Is
The phenomena
This library is about the hard problem of consciousness, the one self-evident and inexplicable phenomenon, and about the inexplicable phenomena that stand beside it in other fields. It is the public library of the repository that bears its name, bound from its source and published as it stands.
What stands here
A book here is a project, or a catalogue of projects. The people who work on these phenomena keep their own account in The Team, and whoever stands in it may author. The subjects their work falls into are catalogues, Mathematics the first, each to hold many books. The library is built with The Manual, the least it needs to bind, and its look is being sketched rather than settled.
