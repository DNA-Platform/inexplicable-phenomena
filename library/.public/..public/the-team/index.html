The question this book opens on
When this library was started, on the night of 2026-10-29, its librarian was asked whose autobiography it would hold, and he answered that it describes the team of people who work on inexplicable phenomena, and then: "Wait, we don't know who qualifies as a teammate. That might be the subject of the book." So it is. This book is about the team, and its first question is who belongs to it.
What we think, so far
Arthur: a teammate is someone whose work on these phenomena stands in this library under their own name, which means a book filed under this one. Nobody qualifies by being listed; they qualify by what they have written here.
Libby: and the library's own rule agrees, since a book filed under the autobiography may author. Whoever is filed here can write the subjects they keep, and whoever is not, cannot. The question is settled book by book, not once.
Cathy: the one who built the framework these ideas are painted in counts, and so does the one whose ideas they are. Whether the librarian himself stands here as a teammate, or above it as the one who vouches for everyone, is his to say.
Nancy: I would add a test from the work itself. Someone qualifies when they have asked a question about an inexplicable phenomenon in a way that could be answered, and have taken an answer they did not want. The rest is bookkeeping.
