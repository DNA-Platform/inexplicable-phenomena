Referential Objectivity

This one should discuss what is unique to Mathematics. The textbook is." The other four are kinds any subject keeps and stand in the library's own manual as The Books; what remains here is the textbook, whose results are stated and proved in an algebraic form, and whose entry is unspecified since the apparatus that drew it was struck on the same word.
A part for the forms stood here from my rulings of 12 November 2026 — the specification of a specification, and the content whose front is the deliverable and whose back is the project — and was struck whole on 21 November, to be specified again from nothing; the subject's deliverable wore a theorem and a proof of its own there, and they are struck with it, never having been said in a chapter. This manual holds no code.

The Biography of Subjective Identity
The Biography of Subjective Identity
A blog of mathematical insights, written in the subject's own voice. It was always meant to be one: a subject's account of its own formalization, in the first person, under the pseudonym of mathematics itself. The posts carry my insights and mathematics speaks them, and the switch of perspective between the two of us is not built yet — it comes later, on my word of 21 November 2026.
What the blog is about, beyond the mathematics: how much more accessible mathematics has become in the wake of AI. Results that would have taken a season of reading now take an afternoon of asking; a proof assistant that once demanded a specialist now answers an amateur; and a subject that was reached through its guild is reachable through a conversation. That is the change the posts keep returning to, and the first of them is about what it is like to work that way.
