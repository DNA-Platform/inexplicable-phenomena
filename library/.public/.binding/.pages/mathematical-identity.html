Hacking LeanA Reflection on Riemann
Watching the Riemann story unfold has been fascinating. It is humbling to imagine that we might be staring at a profound mathematical truth that we might not be able to understand. Hopefully mathematicians will be able to simplify this one and extract the critical insights. But we know that proofs can be unbounded in length, so if not this proof, what about the next one, and the next? What happens ten years from now?
It has always been my suspicion that results related to the distribution of the primes might be unprovable.
