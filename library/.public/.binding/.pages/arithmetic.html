The Textbook of Arithmetic
The Textbook of Arithmetic
The book holds the official results of arithmetic, stated and proved, and a result enters only as I have ruled it. Its official form is the algebraic one. It carries no chapters on my word of 21 November 2026 and it is to be specified again from nothing.
Its own specification of 12 November 2026, amended on 15 and 20 November, is retired to the subject's notes, where it stands as my first specification of this book and records the three retirements it had reached. Nothing of the abstraction that carried it is kept: the form of a specification and the content that bore it were struck from the library's manuals on the same word, and the next attempt begins in the main library.

The Notebook of Arithmetic
