The Preface
The paper as it stood when its flow was last read back, on 5 November 2026: titled Gödel Through Goldbach then, The Subject of Arithmetic now, Gödel first and ending on Goldbach's conjecture. Gödel's theorems leave arithmetic a choice, add G, add ¬G, add consistency and its successors, or change nothing. Mathematics changed nothing and never said why. The paper says why, shows that the justification carries a model of arithmetic, and shows that in that model one sentence of number theory is derived that PA cannot prove, so it enters PA as an axiom. Doug's ruling that set this order, on 29 October: "You miss the idea that it is the Gödel result, and how we justify not changing PA that is the result that follows you around to every single formalism. Granting that argument is granting the model of arithmetic involved" (Goldbach through Gödel, The Surviving Sentence, 29 October 2026).
Two notions reimagined
Consistency is not a proposition but the inferential identity, the empty proof ε, which no sentence can be; and incompleteness is not meaningful in these semantics. His words of 31 October, which turned the preface: "A proposition and its negation are both undecidable in the semantics when they are expressed by the same (empty) proof, and are therefore not representable by the subject" (Goldbach through Gödel, The Six Movements, 31 October 2026); and the same evening, "Something and its negation having the same consequence is an expression not expressed in this subject"; "Consistency itself is not an axiom, and it does not compel adding additional ones that are out of the subject. Hence, we feel no need to add G even if we imagine it is true" (The Six Movements). Gödel's result read this way is a result about representation, not a defect of PA: there are empty representations, and the negation of an empty representation is the same empty representation. The subject is complete over what it represents. This deprecates the earlier frame in which the paper reinterpreted incompleteness as a theorem about PA's gaps; after 31 October there is no gap to reinterpret.
Where it ends
"Let's call it Gödel through Goldbach. Where do we start? I think we end on GC" (The Six Movements, 31 October 2026). The inferential identity is the Gödel half; Goldbach is the last line and not an aside, his correction of the first draft that had it the other way round: "Haha you think establishing an inferential identity is the thing, with a justification for the oldest unsolved math conjecture as an aside?" (The Two Drafts, 28 October 2026). What the result is and is not: "You understand that GC as axiom is revolutionary. De Polignac is great but if you are doing a condensed version, that is not a part of the result at all. It is a nice corollary" (The Surviving Sentence). The whole has exactly one new act, the adoption of a sentence that survives the loss of its proof, said in The Mechanism; remove it and the paper proves nothing about Goldbach and claims nothing.
The genesis, for the motivation
How the result was found is told once, in a message Doug wrote for Gemini and never sent, on 4 November: a nightmare about the number every prime divides, the way out through zero, and the discovery that the way out had been modular arithmetic all along. "I always felt mathematicians were tricky. They play little games with your mind, making you hallucinate things on the edge of possibility and try to convince you that they are real" (Subjectivity library catalogue, the unsent coda, 4 November 2026). "And the magic is that I didn't prove anything. I simply say look at that result that isn't in PA but is true on things arbitrarily large" (the unsent coda). And to Gemini, the day before, the stance the paper offers the reader: "This is a subjective alternative for PA provided you have no problem with Con(PA) being unprovable because it is unformalizable" (Subjectivity library catalogue, the Gemini transcript, 3 November 2026). The coda is the preface's motivation if the paper carries one; it is not part of the formal flow.
The title's history
Coined in October 2025 as Goldbach through Gödel and flipped the same sitting: "Shouldn't it be Gödel through Goldbach? Because we are using Goldbach to express unprovability? Or are we reflecting on the unprovability of Goldbach using Gödel as a lens?" (Gödel through Goldbach: The Paper, Memory Exhaustion, 12 October 2025). Carried a year later as Goldbach Through Gödel for the working drafts of 26 to 29 October, flipped again on 31 October as above, and fixed as Gödel Through Goldbach at the read-back of 5 November. Named The Subject of Arithmetic here on 9 November 2026 when this notebook was made, the subject being what the paper is about: a theory read as a subject that expresses its consistency by what it cannot say.
The Object
The paper has one object, 0, which PA proves is the unique number every prime divides, and it has three names. In Gödel's chart it is the proof of every length, which with length 0 free is the empty proof. In the sieve it is the horizon, the top of every primorial circle. In Goldbach it is the pairless number, the only structural counterexample. Mathematics already identified the three; the paper reads what the identification says (Goldbach through Gödel, The Paper's Flow, 5 November 2026). The three names are the three movements Zero for Proof, Zero as the Horizonand Zero as the Counterexample.
Zero as absence and as smallest
The tension the object resolves was stated on 26 October: "There is tension with zero. Should it be a quantity when it means the absence of one. Should it be an index of a number of fingers that means before the fingers?" (Goldbach through Gödel, Proof of Length Zero, 26 October 2026). The paper's answer is that the two semantics are two subjects of one domain: zero as absence, where a sentence with the empty proof is unprovable, and zero as smallest, where it is an element. "Gödel's ignores a proof length of zero and ours uses it to encode the absence of a proof - that is something and its negation are the same because each interpretation represents the same semantic concept but represented in different subjects" (The Two Semantics of Zero, 1 November 2026). The same reading given to Gemini, in the words he marked as a huge change: "Zero turns an absence of ontological object into the presence of absent object to serve as the identity ontological consistency" (Subjectivity library catalogue, the Gemini transcript, 3 November 2026).
The empty proof is zero
Where the empty proof lives was decided on 26 October against Gödel's product coding, which puts it at 1: "The empty proof should be the zero for proofs. And furthermore, every zero length proof simply must be the same" (The Two Zeros, 26 October 2026). And the day after, the ruling that settled its address for good, lengths are quantities and codes are names: "Remember that you need numbers to be quantities for 0 to make sense. Your example also has a problem because the proof is represented by the number in code. But the length of the proof is a quantity associated with it" (The Lifted Arithmetization, 27 October 2026). The chart agrees: a proof of every length would be divisible by every prime, and the number every prime divides is 0.
The horizon is zero
"No, the horizon is 0 - the unreferable thing is like the predecessor of the limiting primorial. But any expression of an infinite thing will have an infinite predecessor too" (The Two Semantics of Zero, 1 November 2026). And in the argument of 1 November that fixed the method: "were there a horizon (an infinity) it would be 0, and we can only see one side of the loop (the number to the left of zero cannot be referred to)" (The Resolution, 1 November 2026). This deprecates the far point as a second zero, raised on 28 October and set out of the domain on 1 November; there is one horizon, it is 0, and the unreferable is its predecessor.
The counterexample is zero
The question that opened the circle on 26 October: "Does it change anything to imagine that zero is that number divisible by all primes, and it fails to be a Goldbach counterexample if small. Does that way of imagining things help make GC seem true" (The Circular Arithmetic, 26 October 2026). It does: a number every prime divides is even and pairless, and PA names it 0, out of the conjecture's range, so the witness of ¬G and Goldbach's only structural counterexample are one number. That identification is the third movement.
The Mechanism
The kind of result the paper claims, stated by Claude on 29 October and kept on Doug's order: "A theorem at every finite stage whose proof uses a property every stage has and the limit lacks. The proof doesn't survive the limit, because it used the property. The sentence survives, because it never mentioned it. A sentence that survives the loss of its proof is an axiom" (Goldbach through Gödel, The Surviving Sentence, 29 October 2026). His word on it: "this is very good. Note this in the memory of this or in a document", being "a good description of how we arrive at an axiom that has the computationally checkable property that we expect but isn't generalizable" (The Surviving Sentence). It is the one document of the three that the paper rests on, The Type of Result, saved that day.
Choosing an axiom, not proving a theorem
The method is a technique for choosing an axiom, and Doug refused every reading that made it a proof: "You understand that we are inventing a technique for choosing an axiom, NOT proving theorems?" (The Two Sieves, 28 October 2026); "I can't tell you enough that if you think you are proving GC you are just wrong"; "We were trying to superimpose a property that isn't derivable" (The Circle Proof, 28 October 2026). The definition of an axiom of arithmetic the work reached on 25 October still stands under it: "A sentence that I believe is true that no combination of the axioms can decide is a good definition for an axiom of arithmetic" (The Shape of the Phantom Proof, 25 October 2026). The adoption rule is the paper's one new act, and The Surviving Sentence is where it is performed.
The identification
Behind the type is an identification: an imagined model identifies two things the theory cannot state as equal, and the only trace of the identification in the theory is a theorem changed in form. In his words the equality of the two sieves is "something we cannot state in the limiting structure that is N, so that its consequence is reflected as a change to a theorem" (The Geometric Precedent, 28 October 2026). The criterion a precedent must meet: "We need something that wasn't interesting before the equality, and after the equality is an important fact" (The Geometric Precedent), on which Desargues was rejected. And the simple form he accepted with "It could be an axiom" (The Geometric Precedent): what is a theorem on every finite member becomes an axiom on the limiting member, because the limit being a member is not a theorem.
The precedents from geometry
"It has to be from geometry because that is where mathematicians reasoned their way to axioms like we are" (The Geometric Precedent, 28 October 2026). The precedent the paper carries is Euclid's fifth postulate as the limit of Girard: on every sphere the angle sum minus π is the area over the radius squared, proved by three lunes and the sphere's total area; the plane sheds the closure, no total area, the proof gone, the statement kept, and excess equal to zero is Euclid V. Lambert read the acute case off a sphere of imaginary radius; Lobachevsky adopted the other postulate outright and built the geometry on it, which is the precedent for reasoning about what an axiom is, added on 10 November 2026 on Doug's word; Beltrami showed V unprovable; Hilbert showed the imaginary sphere no object in Euclidean space, having proved the axioms of geometry independent of one another in his Grundlagen. The Riemann sphere and the fundamental theorem of algebra are kept as the field's version, where the limit is an object and the sentence is also a theorem of analysis: "your example is good too. It is. We might give both" (The Geometric Precedent). Dedekind on continuity and Cauchy on Poncelet's principle stand as the precedents for how such adoptions are received (The Surviving Sentence, 29 October 2026).
The thesis behind the method
What makes the method legitimate is a thesis and not a deduction, and it is named for its circularity: "if deduction is linear reasoning, this method of choosing axioms based on seeing a circle is circular reasoning - not usually considered valid, but this is not a valid deduction. And assigning it to my first (last is too fleh) name gives you that beautiful sense that circular reasoning should be as subjective as it is invalid. Yet... we suspect that it stands up to science!" (The Circular Reasoning Thesis, 27 October 2026). Its model: "Any universals that can be proven should literally be machine checkable. The Doug's Circular Reasoning Thesis should be like the Church Turing Thesis" (The Circular Reasoning Thesis). The paper states it in Church and Turing's form, with criteria and a refutation clause, as the ninth step of the flow of 31 October and the close of movement Goldbach.
Zero for Proof
The first movement: proof is a quantity, and the semantics mathematics already uses for an absent quantity, 0 as the identity, is applied to it. "True but unprovable" is the identity read as a value. The movement was built from 26 October to 3 November in four forms that are one object, the zero trick, Proof(n, 0), the inferential identity and the lifted predicate Pf(φ, ℓ) with 0 the unique number of every length; the paper uses the last, and this note states that form, citing the earlier ones as its history.
Proof as quantity
Derivations as finite sequences with concatenation and the empty sequence ε; length a homomorphism onto the naturals with the length of ε equal to 0; and the axiom rule as line 1, so length 0 is unassigned until the semantics assigns it. Doug's seed, 26 October: "Imagine Proof predicate with a witness that is proof length"; "Internalize provability. What happens if you reflect on provability with this predicate?" (Goldbach through Gödel, Proof of Length Zero, 26 October 2026). His ruling that the length and not the code carries the semantics: "you need numbers to be quantities for 0 to make sense" (The Lifted Arithmetization, 27 October 2026). The lifted predicate of 31 October defines the proof of length 0 as having no other length, Pf(φ, 0) := ∀ℓ ≥ 1 ¬Pf(φ, ℓ), so that in these semantics the proof of length 0 is unprovability (The Six Movements, 31 October 2026).
The identity and its lemmas
ε adjoined to the sentences as the empty proposition, with X ∧ ε = X, X ∨ ε = X, ¬ε = ε and π ⊕ ε = π, not a lattice element, distributivity not asserted at it. Doug named it on 28 October, "SO then it's the inferential identity!" (The Identity Element, 28 October 2026), and on 3 November corrected its level: "You don't even understand that empty would be the axiomatic identity too?" (The Formal Core, 3 November 2026), so ε is the identity at every level, string, sentence, deduction, axiom and theory, and T + ε = T because ε was already an axiom of T. Two lemmas make the movement formal. The Neutrality Lemma: T + C is conservative over T if and only if T proves C, so no sentence can be consistency and the progression of consistency statements is ε added to ε. The Same-Consequence Lemma: in a consistent subject no represented sentence has the same consequences as its negation, so a pair with equal consequences is not something the subject expresses (The Six Movements, 31 October 2026).
Gödel, read
PA ⊢ G ↔ Pf(G, 0); PA ⊢ Con ↔ Pf(⊥, 0); PA ⊢ Pf(Con, 0) ↔ Con; and PA does not prove Con → Con(PA + Con), so Gödel's Con does not carry consistency across the addition of a true sentence and is not a formalization of consistency. Doug's test, 27 October: "his Con can't be my Con. Mine captures the essence of Con(PA) and his fails the transitivity of Con(PA). He never formalized it" (The Lifted Arithmetization, 27 October 2026). His semantics of consistency, said back as definition: "Con(Th) is the consistency of the first order theory you are in is the zero length proof, and the thing that implies all of the axioms. We chose them in an attempt to describe a consistent vision of something" (The Standard Reading, 27 October 2026). By Same-Consequence, G and ¬G both have consequence ε and are not represented; incompleteness is not meaningful here. No G is added: imagining G true is imagining Pf(G, 0), already a theorem, and adding is leaving the subject. The example the paper carries: PA + ¬Con resolves to PA, since its witness is 0 and Pf(⊥, 0) is Con.
One sentence, two subjects
The theorem at the center of the movement: PA ⊢ Pf(φ, 0) ↔ ¬Prov(φ), one sentence of the theory and two meanings of it, absence as an element and absence as a negation, and PA cannot tell which it is in (The Two Semantics of Zero, 1 November 2026). Doug: "this is it!! That sentence depends on what you believe is the right proof predicate, and the same encoding can express both based on proof length" (The Two Semantics of Zero). Earlier, on 26 October, the same fact in its first form, "It is expressible. Prov(X,0) and Prov(not X,0) is incompleteness" (Proof of Length Zero, 26 October 2026), and on 28 October, "if all empty proofs prove the same thing (Con(PA)) then G and not G are the same statement. That is different. That is cool" (The Identity Element). The frontier results of 3 November belong here too, Theorem A first: Pf(S, 0) is never provable, the identity used in every proof and concluded in none, which is the formal content of consistency motivating every step and being proved by none (The Formal Core, 3 November 2026). The rest of that core is listed in The Documents.
Zero as the Horizon
The second movement: the model the stance of staying in PA carries. In Gödel's chart the proof of every length is divisible by every prime, so it is 0, the top of the lengths is their origin. The domain is the numerals, referential integrity, held in the host and never in PA. The numbers are one-sided, no mirror, no predecessor of 0. And the stance believes itself flat because it cannot measure its distance to the horizon (Goldbach through Gödel, The Paper's Flow, 5 November 2026).
The flatness axiom
PA proves that no successor is 0, and that axiom is what forces the domain infinite; remove it and every finite ring is a model of what remains. Doug's argument of 1 November: "Do you believe that numbers are the completion of a set that was always closed under addition and multiplication? Until you said 0 has no successor, your expression of closure of + and * meant you believed in that. Well then forcing the fact about 0 means extending the right to have an infinite domain" (Goldbach through Gödel, The Resolution, 1 November 2026). So the one axiom about 0 is the flatness axiom, and the belief under it is the flatness belief: "I cannot measure my distance to the horizon, so I am flat. V holds for every triangle you can measure and the plane may still be curved; S(x) ≠ 0 holds for every number you can name and the line may still close" (The Paper's Flow, 5 November 2026).
No predecessor of zero
Ruled on 29 October against a predecessor of 0 kept as an unnamed thing with an address: "But there is no predecessor of 0. It says it in the axioms. And it turns out if you want to understand the PA we were imagining, negative numbers don't actually make sense" (The Flatness Belief, 29 October 2026). The group structure of the finite rings, inverses, a mirror, a predecessor of 0, is the one feature the limit sheds: "taking the limit of modular arithmetics is taking the limit of arithmetics where addition was already closed, and the predecessor of 0 was a bit of an aberration" (The Surviving Sentence, 29 October 2026). This deprecates the field's closure, the projective line and the Riemann sphere as the picture, set aside the same day as the field's version of the act and not ours.
Referential integrity
Nothing is in the domain unless a term built from 0 and S refers to it. Its logical form is the ω-rule; it is not first-order, by compactness; it holds in exactly the standard model, the only model of PA with computable addition and multiplication by Tennenbaum; nonstandard models fail it and are not rival interpretations (The Surviving Sentence, 29 October 2026). It is a semantic assertion and not an axiom, and Doug's ruling of 1 November made it a naming and not an exclusion: "We did that because we don't want to name any number that can't be expressed with successors of 0. And we are back to PA. But we came back to a PA that has a modularity that the last one didn't, even though it is identical, which means we must need additional axioms to explain the semantic change we just made" (The Resolution, 1 November 2026). The ℕ this fixes is the ℕ in ℝ; granting Gödel's own argument that G is true grants it. How the horizon is resolved to 0 is The Surviving Sentence.
Not a contradiction
When Claude framed the thesis as a contradiction selecting an axiom, Doug refused it: "I don't think ours is a contradiction" (The Flatness Belief, 29 October 2026); the picture is a limit of modular arithmetics in which we must believe we are flat, and the thing all primes divide being zero was already in the theory. And against a lecture on ω-inconsistency: "I am insulted. I am reinterpreting Gödel" (The Flatness Belief). The view is not ω-inconsistent: the limiting primorial's predecessor is not named, its unnameability noted, and its successor taken as 0.
Zero as the Counterexample
The third movement, Gödel through Goldbach proper: resolving ¬G and removing Goldbach's counterexample are the same act. A number every prime divides is even and pairless, so the witness of ¬G and the structural counterexample to Goldbach are one number, and PA names it 0, out of range. Goldbach gives Gödel's unpointable object its only arithmetic face (Goldbach through Gödel, The Paper's Flow, 5 November 2026).
Two lemmas in PA
L1: PA proves that the number every prime divides is 0, which was already in the theory and is Euclid's theorem read in modular dress, since for every nonzero x there is a prime not dividing x. L3, pairlessness: PA proves that if every prime divides x then x is not a sum of two primes, since x = p + q gives p dividing q, so x = 2p, which some prime fails to divide (The Surviving Sentence, 29 October 2026). Doug's statement of L3 on 1 November: "For an even, if p divides it, N-p can't be prime, so no two standard primes could add to the limiting primorial" (The Resolution, 1 November 2026); and of its place in the paper on 29 October: "It is noteworthy that a large value that is divisible by all primes is a GC counterexample" (The Surviving Sentence, 29 October 2026).
The number he feared
The identification was his from the fourth day, held against refutation: "Why can't that number, ignoring the fact that we think it causes inconsistency, be an even number where every prime can't have a prime pair to sum because it divides it?" (The Shape of the Phantom Proof, 25 October 2026); and the reason it recurs, "each time we recurse and arithmetize, we believe in that number again, only to see it would cause inconsistency and is not provable. GC becomes another way to assert the inconsistency" (The Shape of the Phantom Proof). Claude's arithmetization answered the demand "can you arithmetize that the proof of G contains a Goldbach counterexample?" (The Shape of the Phantom Proof): PA proves Gödel's sentence equivalent to the sentence that any proof of it contains a Goldbach counterexample. In the coda to Gemini the same number is the nightmare: "that scary number fails the GC criteria. It is divisible by 2, and all of the standard primes divide so if one were to imagine that two might divide it, you can prove that they don't" (Subjectivity library catalogue, the unsent coda, 4 November 2026).
Deprecated by the third movement
His first use of the circle, on 25 October, had the top of the circle break Goldbach where it is in fact easiest; the claim survives only under his rigid reading of prime, which prices the axiom as Goldbach plus every number being standard (The Finding of an Axiom, 25 October 2026). The hinge, whether PA proves GC → Con(PA), raised on 19 October and found to be nobody's theorem on 23 October, is not a premise of the paper; what it reached for is L1 and L3, where the witness of ¬G and the counterexample meet without an implication between the conjecture and consistency. And the phantom of 25 October, the all-primes number as a nonstandard element, is in the paper a name, 0, and no nonstandard element at all, by the ruling of 31 October that removed nonstandard-model language from the exposition. How that was done is The Surviving Sentence; what the two sieves prove before it is Two-Sided Arithmetic.
Two-Sided Arithmetic
The fourth movement: ℕ is the limit of groups and is not one. On the rings ℤ/N_k, with N_k the primorial, the survivors of sieving by the first k primes form a group U_k with U = −U; the Chinese remainder theorem counts the pairs summing to any residue, ∏(p − c_p), positive for every even residue; for every center and every depth there is a k with n − k in P and n + k in P′, where P is the primes from below and P′ the same residues read from above, different numbers, 23 and 187; normalized, the count is the Hardy–Littlewood singular series (Goldbach through Gödel, The Paper's Flow, 5 November 2026).
The two sieves
Doug's construction, demanded on 31 October: "an upper and lower sieve argument establishing P and P' - the primes from below and a different but symmetric set from above, have the Goldbach conjecture for finite primorial modular arithmetics - for all n big enough, n-k in P and n+k in P' - uses Chinese remainder theorem I believe" (Goldbach through Gödel, The Six Movements, 31 October 2026). Its first form on 26 October: "those two sets have the same shape. You can't show that for numbers greater than 4 or whatever and less than primorial(N)-4 or whatever, you can find an element of one set and an element of the other set equidistant?" (The De Polignac Identity, 26 October 2026). And the demand for proof over verification on 28 October: "Verification isn't proof. It is important. You need to prove it. Use Chinese remainder theorem or whatever, but provide a proof that should yield the result in the limit" (The Two Sieves, 28 October 2026). Theorem A's pairing count at every center, Theorem B's unwrapped pairing with error never reaching the window, and Theorem T's twin-survivor count were proven that day.
The sets differ at every stage
"The whole point is that the upper sieve DOESN'T produce the same set" (The Two Sieves, 28 October 2026). At full depth the two differ at every finite N, 23 surviving at 210 while its mirror 187 = 11·17 does not. The symmetry is the primitive and the circle only its picture, his correction of 27 October: "It's not actually the circle. It's that the primes have a symmetry - two sets that shouldn't be the same but are. But otherwise it's not the circle. So you pick and choose the facts. I'm worried that you don't see the pattern. It's kind of like axiomatizing different geometries" (The Enlightened Arithmetic, 27 October 2026). The seed of the symmetric form is five months older: "They inherit their symmetry from the fact that the coprimes of N are symmetric" (Gödel through Goldbach: Coprime Symmetry, 30 March 2026).
The barrier and the aberration
Passing from a congruence to an equation in ℤ introduces boundary terms no sieve depth controls well enough to land a pair in the window where a survivor is prime: the sieving limit of dimension two, Jurkat and Richert's lower bound vanishing exactly at full depth, the parity phenomenon under both, Chen's theorem the best unconditional output. The residue theorems do not pass to quantities by proof. What every finite stage has and the limit sheds is the group structure, inverses, mirror, predecessor of 0, one feature (The Surviving Sentence, 29 October 2026). This is why the identification is consistent only at the unreachable limit, and why the paper's axiom is sited at the barrier rather than beyond it. Doug's demand that the whole be said in PA's own language, "You are axiomatizing the limit of circular arithmetics based on a primorial horizon" (The One Axiom, 27 October 2026), is what the passage of the next movement answers.
Deprecated by the fourth movement
The eight earlier forms of the axiom hunted on 27 and 28 October, Generation and Centers, Local–Global, the residual is zero, the Parity Coin, U, S, E and D, each refused as not PA, "you aren't speaking in the language of PA. You are too off in the clouds" (The One Axiom, 27 October 2026); the Mirror Axiom, which prescribes two ends and no center; the enlightened arithmetic C with its axiom list, rejected as model-shopping; the factorial circle, no easier than the primorial; and the profinite limit of the circle proof, "How did you find an uncountable thing as the limit of a bunch of finite algebras" (The Circle Proof, 28 October 2026). The one axiom that remained is the identification itself, performed in The Surviving Sentence.
The Surviving Sentence
The fifth movement, where the one new act is performed. Goldbach is Theorem M minus the thing that proved it. Imagine the nonstandard stage; resolve it by referential integrity, the horizon has one name, 0, and N − p has one referent, p, so P′ collapses onto P; only the sum reading survives in the one-sided language; the proof used the inverses and does not come, the sentence never mentioned them and does (Goldbach through Gödel, The Paper's Flow, 5 November 2026). Three lines each: a theorem on every sphere, the plane is the sphere at infinite radius, Euclid V; a theorem on every circle, the line is the circle at N = 0, Goldbach.
The resolution, in four steps
Doug's ruling of 1 November reorganized the method and stands at the close: "P and P' are always assumed to be different sets. We are looking for properties that we will use to build our idea of the limiting structure, but P and P' the same is not a given. It is a result of a resolution to a particular non-standard model that carries semantic consequence" (Goldbach through Gödel, The Resolution, 1 November 2026). The four steps as Claude then stated them: a family theorem, PA proving the sieve statement on every ring from some depth on, with the two sets never identified; the imagined nonstandard stage, where the family theorem holds verbatim at a stage that does not exist and the modulus is divisible by every standard prime; the resolution by referential integrity, under which that modulus has exactly one name, 0, and every description that loses its referent is re-read, so P′ collapses onto P not because the sets were equal but because the resolution leaves the description one referent; and the return to PA, the identical theory, now holding a picture it cannot see, recorded by adding the sentences it does not decide. "The limiting primorial = 0 resolution imagines a nonstandard model, finds our way back because the number divisible by all primes is zero uniquely" (The Resolution).
The passage and the adoption
The passage τ from the two-sided language to the one-sided one drops −x and reads N − x as the index x; only the sum reading survives, since the one-sided language has no minus, which is why GC and not a difference statement is the trace. τ of the symmetric pairing theorem is Goldbach's conjecture in symmetric form, and P′ = P is a description collapsing onto its only referent by flatness and L1. It is not a theorem, the proof used the inverses and the barrier is its shadow. Then the adoption rule R: a sentence that survives the loss of its proof is an axiom (The Six Movements, 31 October 2026). Doug on what the act is and is not, 28 October: "The beauty of my technique is the DIFFERENCE between the sets. Goldbach
The sixth movement and the last line. Axiom: for every n at least 2 there is a k at least 0 with n − k and n + k both prime. Π₁; every instance checkable, none generalizable; PA plus Goldbach consistent exactly when Goldbach is true; verified to 4·10¹⁸, with Helfgott's ternary case the nearest theorem. Subjective consistency
"That which is not legible within the subject expresses consistency by not being provable" (Goldbach through Gödel, The Six Movements, 31 October 2026). Stated as the belief it is: that PA does not prove GC is not known and not needed.
The refutation clause
"Therefore if GC proves false by check in PA, we deem PA + GC inconsistent (it would be false there too) because we added GC as an axiom to express our belief that PA did not express the criterion necessary to express its proof" (The Six Movements, 31 October 2026). A counterexample is a mistake about numbers and never only a picture, his ruling of 1 November on the sentence that had made it one: "That is a sentence iff this work is not about numbers. Yet the point I am making is that if you believe that those conjectures are true statements about numbers (you think prime numbers are numbers right) and you believe that those conjectures are true (for various reasons involving checking and other analytical number theory results), then you might believe that the numbers of arithmetic always were the completion of modular primorials" (The Resolution, 1 November 2026). So the picture claims the numbers are the completion of the closed modular arithmetics with the horizon named 0, GC is a sentence about numbers that this claim yields, and a counterexample refutes the claim arithmetically, exactly as a measured triangle refutes Euclid V for space.
Conjectures as the record of a belief

The thesis
Granting Gödel's argument grants the model; the model is the standard ℕ, the one in ℝ; the stance of staying in PA, taken by everyone, is the flatness belief, and the flatness belief has one sentence in number theory. This chapter states the model in his words of 9 November, the newest statement of the formalism, gathers the earlier forms it restates, sharpens or renames, with the day each was first said, and ends with the team's candidate for the compact expression he asked for, marked as the team's and not his, and with what is already in PA for the step after it.
Three identities
"An algebraic model of the subject of logic, where we use the inferential identity, which we might comprehend as the empty proof; the rational identity (what I was calling the propositional identity), which is expressed as the empty string; and its truth value is the logical identity, which represents the absence of logic" (in the room, 9 November 2026). Three levels, proof, expression and value, and one identity at each: the empty proof concludes the empty string, and the empty string is valued at the logical identity, so the three are one object seen from each level. The three were first said to Gemini on 3 November: "My reinterpretation of Gödel is to provide a semantics for a referential identity that happens to conclude the propositional identity whose truth value happens to be the logical identity, and this is how I know how these things can acquire structure"; "these semantics allow me to express proof positively alongside Gödel's as a subjective alternative"; "It is not just unprovable, it is also unformalizable. It exists as the empty string"; and of the value, "the truth value of the sentence that expresses consistency. It is... the logical identity. It represents the idea of being unformalizable in logic" (Subjectivity library catalogue, the Gemini transcript, 3 November 2026). He named the three on 5 November when he quoted that passage back to Claude, "inferential identity, propositional identity, logical identity" (Subjectivity library catalogue, 5 November 2026). The lexicon written later on 3 November had its own column, inferential ε, referential 0, representational the empty string, epistemological Pf(y, 0), "and they are one object in Gödel's chart, which is the chart's whole point" (Claude, The Lexicon, 3 November 2026), without the value. One name has moved twice. On 28 October the empty string was the logical identity, "No, Con(PA) represented by the empty string is the logical identity" (Goldbach through Gödel, The Identity Element, 28 October 2026); on 3 November it became the propositional identity and the logical identity its truth value; on 9 November the propositional identity became the rational identity. The earlier notes are read with the newest names.
The excluded middle kept
The logical identity "does not invalidate the law of the excluded middle because it is not another value, it is a value that corresponds to an expression that specifies that the expression is illogical" (in the room, 9 November 2026). This restates what was settled to Gemini on 3 November, "Have I broken the dichotomy of logic? No... The middle represents that which is logical and the logical identity is not. What it really expresses is that sentences that would be logically equivalent to the propositional identity and might have the logical identity as a truth value express unformalizability - the outside of logic" (Subjectivity library catalogue, the Gemini transcript, 3 November 2026), and closes a question open since the first day. On 19 October the law was doubted: "Consistency is what logic is about"; "you get to truth through proof, as formalized by logic, so there is an undecidable middle" (The Permission, 19 October 2026), and the price of dropping it was named that afternoon, Kripke's, some sentences get no value (Claude, Certainty, 19 October 2026). On 28 October the identity absorbed it: "G and not G have the empty proof, so G and not G are Con(PA) and Con(PA) which is just Con(PA) which we already know anyways" (The Identity Element, 28 October 2026), "Excluded middle holds; it just evaluates to the identity" (Claude, The Identity Element). On 1 November he weighed removal, under the heading of dangerous interpretations: "X and not X being the same expression means the law of the excluded middle must be interpreted only for sentences that are formalizable in the subject, and should therefore be removed unconditionally" (The Two Semantics of Zero, 1 November 2026), and the answer that day was to keep it and note that it has no content at ε. On 3 November he said what a derived identity would be: "Since consistency is the inferential identity, it is a deduction that represents the absence of deducibility, and the semantics of empty deductions is that were they to ever be derived, it would be a sentence on the empty line of a proof that has no characters, which means the deduction couldn't be empty, which means the negation of something provable is provable too - inconsistency" (Mathematical Relativity, 3 November 2026). The statement of 9 November says it in the model's own terms: the identity is not a third value, so bivalence is untouched; it is the value of an expression the subject does not represent, and what it says of that expression is that it is illogical. The removal of 1 November is deprecated; the price of 19 October is not paid.
Rules of inference and the axioms on the page
"We want rules of inference that allow consistency as rational identity to motivate writing axioms on the page, which is the constructive way of saying they don't need a reason but also that it is our belief in the consistency of our formalization of the subject which compels us to use axioms to identify the objectives of the subject. Logical objectives identify the conclusions of the subject" (in the room, 9 November 2026). The axiom rule as the identity's rule is the oldest piece of the model, 27 October: "the inference rule is Con(PA) => axiom (abstractly) so it being on the proof is what justifies adding axioms anywhere. We added those axioms because we think we are describing a consistent thing"; "as a rule of inference, Con(PA) => axiom, which reads empty => axiom, which is perfectly valid" (The Lifted Arithmetization, 27 October 2026); and consistency as "the thing that implies all of the axioms" (The Standard Reading, 27 October 2026). The identity's other rules were found on 28 October and found to be one rule: "The real trick is that you did nothing. You can add rules of inference for the empty sentence. And it to anything. Or it with anything. If you can imagine it as a line on the proof (that adds no lines to the proof), negate it... all of these things leave everything unchanged. You effectively just rewrote the previous line, as if repeating a past inference" (The Identity Element, 28 October 2026); every rule for the identity is restate, and the proof of consistency is a repetition. On 2 November the belief behind the rule: "believing in consistency motivates just about every single thing you do in a deduction, and it precedes all deductions because to use deduction is to believe you can reason consistently, so using deduction to prove the consistency of the formalism you are using is to use circular reasoning so it can't be provable" (The Formal Core, 2 November 2026). What 9 November adds is the two readings of one rule, constructive and compelled, and the objectives. The team reads the last sentence so: the axioms identify what the subject is about, its objectives; the logical objectives, what logic itself aims at, identify what follows, its conclusions. That reading is ours and waits on his word.
Soundness as formalizability
"Hopefully we can express the semantics of logic in an algebraic form. Soundness is just the statement that the subject is formalizable" (in the room, 9 November 2026). Soundness here is not a sentence of the subject about itself, Prov(φ) → φ, which on 19 October was listed among the beliefs a subject cannot internalize, beside finite proof length and the law of the excluded middle (Gödel's Corner, 19 October 2026), and whose point he had asked after that morning, "isn't that the whole point of proof and soundness and thus logic?" (Certainty, 19 October 2026). It is the statement that the subject has a formalization at all. Formalizable was given its meaning on 2 November: "For it to be formalizable is to have certain relationships to extended theories - we will prove consistency unprovable iff unformalizable because it would lack the ability to be added as an axiom in a related theory" (The Formal Core, 2 November 2026), and its test the next day: "If consistency were formalized, we could append it to the current theory and that next theory would be consistent iff the previous one is. It doesn't satisfy that so it is not formalized" (The Formal Core, 3 November 2026). To Gemini the stance was offered on that condition, Con(PA) "unprovable because it is unformalizable" (Subjectivity library catalogue, the Gemini transcript, 3 November 2026), and on 4 November the identity was said of subjects at large, "Consistency is still the identity of the subject expressed through semantics"; "Subjects have identities realized as a belief in their consistency that might not be formalizable but can be thought of as the thing that is contradictory between incompatible subjects" (The Subject of Physics, 4 November 2026). So soundness is the identity law holding at every level, string, sentence, deduction, axiom and theory, T + ε = T of Zero for Proof; the unformalizable is what cannot be added as an axiom anywhere, and the only such thing is the identity.
The semantics are the identities
"The interesting thing about those semantics is that the semantics are expressed through the identities, which all more or less mean no change in the interpretation of the formalism other than expressing consistency with the empty proof. So that we see a more-or-less equivalent expression of logic except that consistency is not formalizable in the semantics" (in the room, 9 November 2026). The model adds no semantics beside its identity laws, and each law, at the proof, the expression and the value, says the same thing: the interpretation is unchanged. The one thing the laws express that logic did not is consistency, as the empty proof and what it concludes. So the subject of logic proves what logic proves and says one thing logic does not, that its consistency has an expression and no formalization. This is the form every earlier statement was reaching for. On 27 October the zero was something "you can add to any proof and leave the number of lines intact", and the whole was "my positive way of saying - you can't prove consistency even though you do, in fact, have to assume it" (The Zero Semantics of FOL, 27 October 2026), with the defense "I am just adding PA's zero semantics to FOL so it can host PA" and its gloss, nothing else changes, every theorem still a theorem (Claude, The Zero Semantics of FOL). On 28 October, "The real trick is that you did nothing" (The Identity Element, 28 October 2026). On 31 October the equivalence got its name in the Neutrality Lemma, T + C conservative over T if and only if T proves C, so the one sentence that can be added and change nothing is the one no theory proves, and "Consistency becomes one conservative expression of the underlying identity" (Claude, The Six Movements, 31 October 2026). On 1 November, "The identity is the expression of what nothing means"; "imagining zero as the additive identity is imagining that zero is what we use to denote that something that is not in the subject has no impact on the subject" (The Two Semantics of Zero, 1 November 2026). On 2 November the end of the program was stated as PA itself, "like PA with the addition of an empty theorem to express the absence of provability, that does not add an extra theorem to the language" (The Formal Core, 2 November 2026), and on 3 November the two faces of the one predicate, "We now get two proof predicates that are effectively equal but one expresses provability differently: one logical: not provable, and one representational: the empty proof"; "identities are what leave things unchanged"; "unprovable <=> unformalizable where unprovable is equivalent for both interpretations and unformalizable only exists in one" (Mathematical Relativity, 3 November 2026). The statement of 9 November says it of logic rather than of PA, and in one sentence: equivalent, except that consistency is not formalizable in the semantics.
A candidate compact expression
The team's candidate, written from his words to be corrected, not his statement. In one sentence, after his sharpening: the subject of logic is logic with its identities written in, each identity law saying that the interpretation is unchanged and the laws together being the whole of the semantics, the one expression they add being consistency as the empty proof, so that the subject proves what logic proves and says what logic cannot, that its consistency is expressed and not formalizable. Unfolded, the subject of logic is three carriers, proofs, expressions and values, each a monoid under its own composition, the concatenation of proofs, the connectives on expressions, the operations on values, each with its identity: the empty proof ε, the empty string, and the logical identity. Two maps join them, conclusion from proofs to expressions and valuation from expressions to values, and each carries the identity to the identity, which is the sense in which the three identities are one. The axiom rule is the identity's rule, from ε conclude A, and every other rule involving the identity is restate; consistency is the rational identity, the expression ε concludes, so the belief in consistency is what licenses the first line and is itself never a line. The values are two, with the logical identity adjoined and not a third; φ ∨ ¬φ is valued ⊤ on every expression the subject represents and at the identity on an expression it does not, which is how the identity says that the expression is illogical. A subject is formalizable when the maps carry its identities to one another, and that is its soundness. What the candidate leaves for him to decide: whether the values are the two-element algebra with the identity outside it, his words of 9 November, or the Boolean algebra with one non-distributing element adjoined, the draft of 28 October; and whether the objectives of the subject are its axioms, as read above.

Zero and Infinity
Riemann entered the paper on 12 November as an application to note and no more, in the sketch of Part Three; the same day Doug worked it further on the desktop app and pasted the conversation into the room with one instruction, "try to summarize these, so that we understand this connection which is important" (in the room, 12 November 2026). This chapter is that summary. It opened on his doubt about the team's picture of the object, every direction out of zero a circle coming back to it: "If we imagine the line going out to infinity hits the x-axis somehow... I'm not sure. My thought was the whole complex plane would be like a torus where every direction comes back to zero, but no I'm not considering going one direction at a time" (on the desktop app, 12 November 2026). The answer separates the two directions by looking at one prime's term in its own terms, and there the picture becomes exact: a torus for each prime, not one for the plane, and the mirror of Riemann's functional equation the exchange of zero and infinity. At each step what is a plain computation and what is his thesis applied is marked, as the conversation marked it.
One prime's term
Take a single prime p and the quantity p to the minus s as s moves over the plane. Moving up or down, the imaginary part, only turns it, a clock hand, and the turning repeats, so in that direction the plane rolls up into a cylinder. Moving left or right, the real part, only changes its size: far to the right it shrinks toward zero, far to the left it grows without bound. So for one prime the plane is a cylinder running from zero at one end to infinity at the other, and the critical line is one circle around it, the circle where the size is one over the square root of p. The mirror is what answers his doubt. Riemann's reflection sends a point at distance d to the right of the line to distance d on the left; in this prime's variable that turns small into large and large into small, exchanging the zero end with the infinity end and leaving exactly one circle fixed, the critical line. Seen through any single prime, Riemann's symmetry is the exchange of zero and infinity, and the critical line is where they balance. The team checked the computation: the size at real part σ is p to the minus σ, the reflection takes σ to one minus σ, so a size r goes to one over p·r, the inversion about the circle of radius one over the root of p; taken with the conjugation that the zeros' symmetry carries, it leaves each point of that circle where it is.
A torus for each prime
His identification finishes the shape. If the far end is zero, the two ends of the cylinder are one place; join them and the cylinder is a torus, around in one direction because the prime turns, around in the other because the far end is the origin: the torus he was picturing, every direction returning, the one he had named in the room as "a sort of torus with no hole because the hole would be zero" (in the room, 12 November 2026). It is not one torus for the whole plane, because the primes turn at rates that never agree, the turning of p repeating every 2π over the logarithm of p and no two primes' logarithms standing in a rational ratio; it is one torus for each prime, all sharing the same mirror, since the reflection about the critical line is the same for every prime. The conversation marked the two steps: the change of variable is a plain computation, and the joining of the ends is the thesis of this notebook applied there, the far end of the numbers indistinct from their origin. So the object of Part Three is exact where the whole-plane picture was vague: a direction at a time is a prime at a time, and what is known of each torus is its symmetry, the circle where zero and infinity balance, and nothing of where it closes. That is the standing of The Mechanism, where the sphere is the field's version and the subject asserts only the symmetry, and of the ruling in Zero as the Horizon that nothing stands before zero.

The two readings of Gödel
The conversation then set the two readings side by side, and the shape of their difference is the formal content this chapter carries toward The Subject of Logic. Where they agree: on every theorem of PA, as a string, including the line that the Gödel sentence and the consistency sentence are provably equivalent in PA, which neither reading adds or removes. Where they contradict: on what one program does. Under Gödel's reading the search for a contradiction runs, takes every step and never halts; under the subjective reading, once a theorem is proven that search takes no step, since consistency is the empty string and its proof the empty proof, and "takes step one" against "takes no step" is a flat contradiction about the same object. And on what one sentence means: for Gödel the arithmetized sentence is consistency, so consistency is a true thing PA cannot prove; for the subjective reading consistency is the empty string, so the arithmetized sentence is something else, and the Gödel sentence is not the consistency sentence. Why both can hold at once: theorems only ever mention runs with a numeral length, the two readings differ only about the run that has none, so no theorem can separate them and the contradiction sits entirely in the undecidable; the two interpretations of PA stand to each other as PA with the Gödel sentence stands to PA with its negation, the same theorems below and inconsistent above. That is his earlier line doing its work one level up: if truth is subjective and two things might be true, it had better be undecidable in the subject. Where the proof lives: in the logic with the identities, the model of The Subject of Logic, where the empty string and the empty proof are terms and can be compared with Gödel's sentence; PA cannot host the proof, and that is the point, not a weakness. Marked as the conversation's and not yet the team's: the equivalence of the Gödel sentence and the consistency sentence in PA is a standard result the team holds from memory, to be checked with the rest; whether "takes no step" is a sentence of the formalism or a reading of one is what Toward PA has still to settle.
What breaks the tie
The conversation first judged by fruits, what each reading goes on to give: Gödel's a true sentence nobody can use, his the far end at zero, the mirror, and a rule that reaches the primes. He corrected the standard: "What breaks the tie is whether PA might be a complete subject or if truth is truly bigger than what its subject can identify: The Subject of Arithmetic" (on the desktop app, 12 November 2026). Judging by fruits is the extrinsic standard, the one Gödel himself proposed for new axioms in 1947, from memory and to be checked; his is intrinsic, asking what the subject is. The two readings are then the two answers to one question. Truth bigger than the subject: there are facts of arithmetic PA's subject cannot identify, the standard model holds them, and incompleteness is a real shortfall; that is Gödel. PA a complete subject: what it cannot decide is not a fact it is missing but the boundary, with the identities sitting on it, the empty proof, the empty string, zero as the far end; that is his. No theorem chooses between them, for the reason of the previous section, and a choice with no theorem behind it is a subjective decision in his sense, one that changes the subject to preserve its meaning. So the title is the argument: The Subject of Arithmetic asserts that arithmetic has one, and that the subject is where truth about it lives, where Gödel's reading needs the opposite, a truth with no subject able to identify it. One consequence for the paper, the conversation's and kept: the fruits still matter, in a different role. The prime conjectures are not the reason to pick the reading; they are what a complete subject looks like from inside, since once nothing is outside, the symmetry has nowhere else to come from. For Part Three that fixes the order of the argument, the object first and the proofs of Goldbach and the rest as its consequence, never the proofs as its evidence.

Formal Logic and Formal Arithmetic: System L and System A
System L is our Formal Logic. System A is our Formal Arithmetic, an extension of System L. Both are first-order in language. They differ from standard first-order logic in one respect: a system is extended not only by signs and axioms but also by rules of inference. System A proves exactly the theorems of Peano Arithmetic.

1. Formal Logic: System L
1.1 Signature
A signature Σ is three finite sets:
constants c;
functions f, each with a number of places n ≥ 1;
relations R, each with a number of places n ≥ 1.
1.2 Language over Σ
Logical signs: ¬ ∨ ∀ = ( )
Variables: v₁, v₂, v₃, … (written x, y, z)
Terms: a variable; a constant; f(t₁, …, tₙ)
Formulas: s = t; R(t₁, …, tₙ); ¬φ; (φ ∨ ψ); ∀x φ
Abbreviations: φ → ψ; φ ∧ ψ; φ ↔ ψ; ∃x φ; s ≠ t
A sentence is a formula with no free variable. Variables range over the individuals of the domain and over nothing else.
1.3 Identifications, proofs, theorems
An identification Γ ⊢ φ is a finite set Γ of formulas (the open assumptions) and a formula φ.
A rule of inference is a finite figure: premises (identifications), a side condition, and a conclusion (an identification), written with metavariables for formulas and terms. In a system with signature Σ the metavariables range over the language over Σ.
A proof is a finite sequence of identifications, each the conclusion of a rule whose premises occur earlier.
φ is a theorem when a proof ends in ∅ ⊢ φ.
1.4 Systems and extension
A system is a triple (Σ, Ax, R):
Σ a signature;
Ax a finite set of sentences over Σ, the axioms, each with a name;
R a finite set of admissible rules of inference, each with a name, containing the nine rules of §1.5.
System L is (∅, ∅, the nine rules). It has no signs of its own and no axioms.
A system extends another when it has all of its constants, functions, relations, axioms and rules, and possibly more. So an extension may add five kinds of thing:
1. constants
2. functions
3. relations
4. axioms
5. rules of inference
Standard first-order logic provides 1 to 4. The fifth is what separates System L from it. There are no axiom schemas: what a schema would say is said by a rule.

Equality Inference states or uses the equality of individuals. Equality is of individuals, not of representations: s = t says two terms have one individual, not that they are one term. 1.6 Admissible rules
A rule is admissible when it is:
finite: one figure with finitely many premises;
decidable: whether given identifications stand in the rule can be checked mechanically.
Why rules may be added: logic lets a system specify a domain. Signs and axioms say what the domain is. A Universal Inference says how something is concluded of all of it. A system that specifies a domain must be able to say how generalizations over that domain are made.
System L specifies nothing about its domain. It has one Universal Inference, Generalization: x was arbitrary.
A rule need not be conservative. It may prove sentences the system did not.
1.7 Facts about System L
Equality. Symmetry and transitivity of = are provable from the two Equality rules, and so is s = t → f(…s…) = f(…t…) for every function, and the like for every relation. No extension owes an equality axiom for a new sign.
Agreement with first-order logic. A system that extends L by signs and axioms only proves exactly the first-order consequences of its axioms.

5. Notes toward a publishable write-up
What to state as definitions and theorems
1. Definitions: signature, language, identification, rule, system, extension, admissible rule, Universal Inference. Number them.
2. Theorem: a system extending L by signs and axioms alone proves exactly the first-order consequences of its axioms. Cite the completeness of natural deduction (Gentzen 1934; Prawitz 1965) rather than reproving it.
3. Theorem: the equality properties of §1.7, with the two short derivations.
4. Theorem: System A and PA have the same theorems, with the sketch of §4 written out in full.
5. Theorem: every theorem of System A translates into a theorem of P. This needs the definability of + and · in P, which should be cited or shown.
6. Theorem: the proof relation of any system with admissible rules is recursive, so Gödel's two properties hold.
What must be made exact
"Finite figure with metavariables" needs a precise form. The cleanest is to define a rule as a primitive recursive relation on codes of identifications and to present each rule by its figure.
Substitution and "substitutable" need their standard definitions, and the tautology test needs its definition of truth assignment.
The numbers for =, + and · must be fixed.
Proof by Contradiction is derivable from Conditional Proof and Consequence. Say so, and say the named proof forms are kept because the families are the point.
What a referee will raise, and should be answered in the text
Induction as a rule is known. It is Gentzen's 1936 formulation. Cite it. Note that the rule must carry open assumptions Γ: for full induction the rule and the schema coincide, though in fragments of arithmetic they are known to differ (Parsons).
PA is not finitely axiomatizable (Ryll-Nardzewski 1952). This is the reason a finite presentation must put induction among the rules. Cite it as motivation.
Adding a recursive rule gives a recursively enumerable theory, and by Craig's theorem every such theory has a recursive first-order axiomatization. So what System L adds is not a larger class of theorem sets. It is a finite presentation. The paper should say this plainly before a referee does.
Gödel's theorems apply to every consistent extension of System A by admissible rules, including the extension by Proof by Symmetry. The paper should say how its reading treats the undecidable sentence of that extended system.
Soundness is an assumption along with consistency, if finite proof is the identification of truth. It is not argued, inside the system or beside it. Admissibility asks only what is formal: finite and decidable.
Sources and conventions
For Gödel 1931, cite the Collected Works, volume I (1986), or van Heijenoort's translation in From Frege to Gödel (1967), which Gödel approved. Meltzer's translation is convenient but is not the scholarly standard. Check §3 against it, in particular the printed form of axiom IV.

Typeset rules as inference figures (premises over a line, conclusion under), not as table rows.

Part One, The Subject of Logic
Closed on 11 November, in five sections. The semantics expressed algebraically: the formal system as an abstract algebra, deduction an operation, identities accrued, first the Boolean algebra with the logical identity adjoined, the inferential identity the proof of the formal identity, which is consistency, whose truth value is the logical identity, something whose truth is outside logic. The arithmetization, agreeing with Gödel on what is provable and what is undecidable and differing in the interpretation of the unprovable. Objective and subjective inconsistency: to formalize a subject is to believe it consistent, undecidability the formal way of saying something is not formalizable in the subject, the subject complete as a subject. The close, his: "different semantics, which loosely correspond to the difference between PA with and without zero, demonstrate that Gödel can be interpreted in two different ways. The historical account
The first movement justifies staying in arithmetic and ends at referential inconsistency. Part One went back to before model theory was born, to Gödel's fixed point, arithmetic interpreting arithmetic: "technically we went back to the time before model theory was born right? That's why we started with Gödel. He decided to use arithmetic to interpret arithmetic as his mathematical fixed point. Model Theory is a reflection of incompleteness - that set theory is the object that represents math and therefore it can be used to interpret all the others. That is the same perspective that holds an omega-complete witness as something one can refer to" (in the room, 11 November 2026). Set theory as the subject that holds the totality as an object, "the subject Gödel helped to formalize which expresses the idea of studying the completed totality... The subject of math includes the study of a form of truth that proof cannot represent. That is the subject of set theory"; Gödel's arithmetic "an object that represents language", his own a subject that believes itself expressive enough to specify itself. Sympathy for Hilbert, who wanted Cantor's paradise complete and required proof finite. The rational number: every base-10 digit ever written, in order, modulo simultaneity, is a rational number, and mathematics imagines things far beyond it. Referential inconsistency defined: "the idea that you can reason hypothetically about something that you can't refer to. Suppose I could refer to it? Then and only then could I prove this. Contradiction! Therefore I can't prove this. That is referential inconsistency. You referred to it in that syllogism"; and "The second you referred to a completed totality of things without a finite account, you referred to something that cannot be described by language" (in the room, 11 November 2026). The diagonal argument as the example, his of 12 November: "the diagonal argument is a hypothetical about writing a list. What if one of those hypothetical lists is an infinite length proof that this technique yields inconsistency? A joke, but not quite. There is an idea that if I can write down a finite hypothetical situation to help understand an infinite number of things, then that transcends infinity. And that's only true if it's something you were allowed to refer to in the first place". The movement ends at the expression of referential inconsistency inside PA, Gödel's own hypothetical, suppose G were provable, as the place his argument refers to what it cannot: "You used the idea hypothetically consider if G was wrong and it was in fact provable, and your arguments became referentially inconsistent right there. That is why G is unformalizable".
The sketch. The essential fact
The second movement states the fact and derives it. His: "what can be referred to sensibly is subjective, so that we draw our inspiration from the idea that infinity is not something that can be referred to sensibly in regard to what can be represented in language, yet the universal quantifier expresses generalization over all, but not that it can be removed and replaced with for none instead. To refer to the totality is to only express things that can be reflected in zero. This symmetry, we will take to be fundamental to the subject of arithmetic" (in the room, 11 November 2026). In arithmetic's terms, "every number having a successor, so that it expresses totality in the absence. Yet we refer to the existence of the absence of quantity as an undifferentiated identity. The factorial interpretation and the rule
The fourth movement, ending on symmetry. The number divided by everything up to N is the factorial-like thing, the product of the primes below N; the one divided by everything is zero, and PA proves it is the only one. His Euclid form of 12 November: "Imagine the form of Euclid's proof is: Assume all primes are the primes less than N - One interpretation: Oh well the product of all of them is a GC counterexample. Have I disproved GC yet? Answer: No, because that number plus one is another prime. Repeat. Second Answer: No because that number is 0 which doesn't apply. You can repeat this same argument to twin primes to show that that number plus one and minus one would both be primes. Do you see how it's a form of induction that would be able to prove something if the assumption that there is a completion wouldn't prove another one. Yet zero has that completion as its divisors. Hence the justification as a rule of inference". Corrected the same day: "neither of those are valid inferences. It just proves that they are very simple theorems related to considering the factorial like thing at the top is finite, and the only reason they are invalid is that that same thing imagines us producing another prime". The direction: "one says that the thing that is a counterexample is pushed off to totality, and if zero isn't a counterexample, neither is that. For twin primes, it means more like we imagine that twin primes extend out to the totality". The commitment: "the thing that was missing was a commitment that the rule by which we refer to the totality at the top is that it is indistinct from zero insofar as we can refer to it in arithmetic. That is definitively not the standard model. It's not cartesian. This is not a rule of inference that we would import into that picture of arithmetic. The subjective choice between subjectivity and incompleteness is the justification for this symmetry". And of the old version: "it was a paper justifying that Goldbach as an axiom expresses something that can't be expressed. I figured out how to encode it as a rule of inference. The wedding and the subject
An unformalizable sentence is always a composite, never the identity of the sentence. A bridesmaid is in every wedding she attends and is never the one the wedding is about. His version names the occasion too: "A bridesmaid can be a component of a wedding, but in a gendered thing that creates an identity out of two opposites, there can be parts of that that are necessary, but have no identity in terms of marriage."

The side of proof
Two sets of rules run through this note, and his ruling is that they need not agree: "we don't need the rules of proof to be the same as the rules of meaninglessness." On the side of proof:
Proven: the last statement of some proof.
Refuted: its structural negation is proven.
Unformalizable: neither. It is never what a proof arrives at.
Nothing here stops an unformalizable statement from being used. It can sit inside a proven statement, as G does in S1 ∨ G; it can be assumed and discharged. What it cannot be is a conclusion — "You can prove something with it, but it cannot ever be proven."
The side of meaning
The logical identity is assigned to the empty string. It is fixed under negation, and a statement composed with its negation yields it.
Where the two meet
The mapping from a proof to its last statement does it without anything extra. An unformalizable statement has no proof that ends in it; the only proof left is the empty one, and the empty proof is sent to the empty string. So the value an unformalizable statement gets is whatever the empty string has: the logical identity. That is Pf(X, 0) from the paper, and G sharing the empty string's value, obtained from the mapping and not laid down separately.
The empty string earns its place the same way, without stipulation: it is a part of every string, by joining, and the whole of none that is a sentence.
The identification of truth
His ruling, the moment the analogy arrived: "It can't be an identity! Yes, this is it. The theorems that have it always have it as a composite. It is never the identity of the sentence. Right here, this is the identification of truth." Stated plainly: a sentence is identified when it is the whole of what some proof arrives at. Truth is being that whole.
T: X is the whole of a theorem.
F: the structural negation of X is the whole of a theorem.
Unformalizable: neither — yet X occurs inside theorems as a component.
Every sentence whatever occurs as a part of some theorem, since X ∨ ¬X is always one, so occurring among the theorems distinguishes nothing. What distinguishes is whether the sentence can stand as the whole. Proof maps a composition to its last statement, and the last statement is a whole sentence: the mapping picks out exactly the wholes, and those are the truths. Everything else is material that truths are made with.

Explosion without an event
The rules of proof can stay as they are, so System A still matches PA string for string, and explosion becomes harmless in a precise way: (X ∧ ¬X) → Y is proven for every Y — something proven with a contradiction — while the contradiction itself is never proven, so the step that would deliver Y never happens. Contradiction implies everything survives as a sentence and never as an event.

System A
Drafted for the textbook in the sprint of the formalization and moved to the notes on Doug's ruling of 12 November 2026: the textbook's chapters follow the entry he is specifying, and this exposition is a note.
System A, A for Arithmetic, is the formal system this work means by Peano Arithmetic: first-order Peano arithmetic, defined so that its logic is the standard one and its arithmetization is Gödel's own. This chapter defines the system, maps it into the system P of Gödel 1931, and states the sense in which both formalize one thing, the Peano axioms, so that PA may name System A without losing Gödel's letter.
The logic
The logic of System A is first-order logic as Leary and Kristiansen specify it in A Friendly Introduction to Mathematical Logic, second edition: the connectives ¬ and ∨ and the quantifier ∀ primitive, with ∧, →, ↔ and ∃ their usual abbreviations; the variables v₁, v₂, and so on, one for each positive integer, written x, y, z informally; the equality symbol = present in every language as a logical symbol; the logical axioms the equality axioms E1 to E3 and the quantifier axioms Q1, (∀x φ)→φtx(\forall x\,\varphi) \rightarrow \varphi^x_t(∀xφ)→φtx​, and Q2, φtx→(∃x φ)\varphi^x_t \rightarrow (\exists x\,\varphi)φtx​→(∃xφ), for t substitutable for x; the rules of inference propositional consequence and the quantifier rules, from ψ → φ to ψ → ∀x φ and from φ → ψ to (∃x φ) → ψ when x is not free in ψ; and a deduction a finite sequence of formulas, each a logical axiom, an axiom of the theory, or the conclusion of a rule from earlier lines. The system is sound and complete for first-order consequence. Enderton's A Mathematical Introduction to Logic specifies the same logic over the same number-theoretic language with different engineering, so the notation here is the literature's, not this work's.

System P and System A
The map from A to P sends each sign to Gödel's: 0 to 0, S to f, ¬ to ~, ∨ to ∨, ∀ to Π, the variables v₁, v₂ and on to the variables of the first type; the abbreviations ∧, → and ∃ to P's dot, ⊃ and (Eu); the equality s = t to P's defined identity; and + and · to addition and multiplication as Principia defines them from the class apparatus. Every theorem of System A maps to a theorem of System P: the six axioms become theorems of P under those definitions, and each instance of the induction schema follows from P's single induction axiom, since comprehension, (Eu) (v Π (u(v)≡φ))(\mathrm{E}u)\,(v\,\Pi\,(u(v) \equiv \varphi))(Eu)(vΠ(u(v)≡φ)), supplies the class of the φ\varphiφs and the axiom applied to that class is the instance. The converse does not hold at full strength: P proves sentences of LA\mathcal{L}_ALA​ that System A does not, the price and the purchase of its classes. The difference between the two systems is one belief, the completed totality of properties, which System P holds as a variable one may quantify and System A refuses, holding induction one formula at a time.
Why PA names System A
Peano Arithmetic is the name of a meaning before it is the name of a system: the axioms of Peano 1889, drawn from Dedekind 1888, the simply infinite system with no element before its base. The system P formalizes that meaning inside the logic of Principia, which Gödel adopted, by his own footnote, only to make the proof definite; System A formalizes the same meaning first-order, and it is the system the literature has called PA since the types fell away. The two share the numbers' signs, the numbering and the arithmetization — what Gödel proved of P he proved of a wide class of systems, System A among them, as his lectures of 1934 carried out — so calling System A by the name PA keeps both of Gödel's letters: the letter of his signs, preserved by the dictionary, and the letter of his theorems, which re-run in A through the same numbered definitions. Where the two differ, the difference is not arithmetic but the one belief named above, and this work's subject is the system that refuses it.
