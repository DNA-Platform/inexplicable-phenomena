What This Library Is
The phenomena
This library is about the hard problem of consciousness, the one self-evident and inexplicable phenomenon, and about the inexplicable phenomena that stand beside it in other fields. It is the public library of the repository that bears its name, bound from its source and published as it stands.
What stands here
A book here is a project, or a catalogue of projects. The people who work on these phenomena keep their own account in The Team, and whoever stands in it may author. The subjects their work falls into are books with catalogues of their own, Chemistry, Mathematics, Pharmacology, Philosophy and Physics, each to hold many books, and this reference catalogues them all, with the Dictionary of the words the library is made of. The library is built with The Manual, the least it needs to bind, and its look is being sketched in The Design rather than settled.
